Privacy Policy

Last updated 22 July 2026

Your health data stays on your phone. We do not operate servers that receive it, we do not sell it, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics providers.

Strux is a training and recovery app. It reads your fitness data from services you already use, such as Strava and WHOOP, and shows you training load, recovery and Hyrox-specific guidance.

Data we access

Only when you explicitly connect a service, and only the categories that service grants:

Strava
Activity history — type, start time, duration, distance, average speed and relative effort — and your name from your public profile.
WHOOP
Daily recovery scores, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, sleep records including stage breakdown, workouts, and your name.

We request read-only access. The app cannot post, edit or delete anything in your Strava or WHOOP account.

Where your data goes

When you connect a service, your device talks to that service directly. Your activity, sleep and recovery data is sent from the provider to your phone and nowhere else.

We do not run an analytics service, advertising SDK, crash reporter or tracking pixel. There is no account to create with us, and we hold no database of users.

Data we store on your device

Access tokens
Stored in the iOS keychain or Android keystore — the operating system's protected credential store.
Fitness data
Held in memory while the app is running and discarded when it closes. The app re-fetches it from the provider on next use.

Deleting your data

Disconnecting a service in the app's Connections screen immediately deletes its stored tokens from your device. Deleting the app removes everything the app holds.

To revoke access at the provider, independently of this app:

  • Strava — strava.com/settings/apps
  • WHOOP — the Connected Apps section of your WHOOP account

Children

The app is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data.

Your rights

Because we hold no copy of your data, requests to access, correct, export or delete it are answered by the source: your data lives on your device and in your Strava or WHOOP account. Disconnecting and deleting the app removes our copy in full, since our copy is only ever the one on your phone.

If you are in a jurisdiction granting rights over personal data — including the UK and EU under GDPR, and California under CCPA — those rights apply. Contact us and we will help you exercise them, though in most cases the fastest route is the provider link above.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with an updated date. If the app begins transmitting data to our own servers, this policy will be updated before that change ships.
