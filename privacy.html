Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 October 2026

Your fitness and health data comes straight from those services to your phone. Our server never stores it. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, we do not send it to any AI service, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Strux is a training and recovery app. It reads your fitness data from services you already use — Strava, WHOOP, Oura and Apple Health — and shows you training load, recovery and race-specific guidance.

Data we access

Only when you explicitly connect a service, and only the categories that service grants:

Apple Health — workouts, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep. Read on your phone only.

Strava — activity history (type, start time, duration, distance, average speed, relative effort, calories) and the name on your Strava profile.

WHOOP — daily recovery scores, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, sleep records including stage breakdown, workouts, and the name on your WHOOP profile.

Oura — sleep and readiness data.

Before you connect Strava, WHOOP or Oura, Strux tells you what it will read, how, and how to withdraw and delete. We request read-only access: Strux cannot post, edit or delete anything in your accounts, and it does not write to Apple Health.

Your account

The first time Strux starts, it creates an anonymous account for your device on our server. It has no name, email address or password — only a random identifier. You do not sign in to create it.

What our server does

Two things only:

  • It holds your anonymous account.
  • It relays sign-in to Strava, WHOOP and Oura. Issuing and renewing your access to those services needs a secret that cannot safely live in an app, so that step passes through our server. The server hands the result straight back to your phone and keeps nothing.

It does not receive, store or process your fitness or health data.

Data kept on your phone

  • Strava data for at most seven days after it was fetched, after which it is deleted — even if you have not opened the app.
  • WHOOP data only while the app is running. It is never saved to storage.
  • Apple Health and Oura data, cached so the app opens with your data.
  • Supplements, routines and sessions you record yourself.
  • Access tokens for connected services, in the iOS keychain.

Strux fetches your recent history fresh from each service when you open it.

Explanations

Some cards offer “Why is this?”. The explanation is worked out on your phone from your own recorded data using fixed rules. Nothing is sent anywhere to produce it.

Crash reports

If Strux crashes, a report may be sent to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so the fault can be fixed. A report contains the app version, your device model and iOS version, and technical details of the error. It does not contain your name, your health data or your IP address.

Who else receives your data

  • Cloudflare hosts our server, and so handles the sign-in relay and your anonymous account.
  • Sentry receives crash reports.
  • Strava, WHOOP and Oura receive requests from Strux for your data, under your authorisation. Strava may monitor and collect data about how Strux uses the Strava API, and may use it for any business purpose, as set out in Strava's API Agreement.

We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers, data brokers or AI services.

Deleting your data

Disconnect a service in the Connect screen: everything Strux holds from that service is deleted from your phone, and Strux confirms when it is done. Our server never held it.

Delete your account in the Connect screen: our server deletes your anonymous account. This cannot be undone.

Delete the app: removes everything stored on your phone. (Your anonymous account on our server stays until you delete it in the app, or ask us to.)

To revoke Strux's access at the source, independently of the app:

  • Strava — https://www.strava.com/settings/apps
  • WHOOP — the Connected Apps section of your WHOOP account
  • Oura — the connected apps section of your Oura account

How long we keep it

On your phone: Strava data seven days, WHOOP data only while the app runs, everything else until you disconnect or delete the app. On our server: your anonymous account until you delete it. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.

Children

Strux is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you — which, on our server, is only your anonymous account. If you are in a jurisdiction granting rights over personal data — including the UK and EU under GDPR, and California under CCPA — those rights apply, and we will help you exercise them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date, and made before the change in the app ships.
