Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 October 2026

Data from Apple Health never leaves your phone. Data from Strava and WHOOP is kept on our server so the app can receive updates even while it is closed. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.

Strux is a training and recovery app. It reads your fitness data from services you already use, such as Strava, WHOOP and Apple Health, and shows you training load, recovery and Hyrox-specific guidance.

Data we access

Only when you explicitly connect a service, and only the categories that service grants:

Apple Health — workouts, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep. Read on your phone only; never sent to our server or anyone else.

Strava — activity history (type, start time, duration, distance, average speed, relative effort, calories) and the name on your Strava profile.

WHOOP — daily recovery scores, heart-rate variability, resting heart rate, blood oxygen, skin temperature, sleep records including stage breakdown, workouts, and the name on your WHOOP profile.

Oura — sleep and readiness data, if you connect it.

We request read-only access. Strux cannot post, edit or delete anything in your Strava, WHOOP or Oura account, and it does not write to Apple Health.

Your account

The first time Strux starts, it creates an anonymous account for your device on our server. It has no name, email address or password — it is a random identifier that lets our server hold your connected data for you. You do not sign in to create it.

Data we store on our server

Our server runs on Cloudflare. For your anonymous account it stores:

  • Access tokens for the services you connect, encrypted, so the server can fetch new data on your behalf.
  • Your provider account name and id**, so incoming updates from Strava or WHOOP can be matched to your account.
  • Activities from Strava, and recovery and sleep** records from WHOOP.
  • How many explanations you request each day**, to apply a daily limit.

It also keeps a technical log of deliveries from Strava and WHOOP (when they arrived and whether they were accepted). These entries contain no health values and are used only to find faults.

Data we store on your phone

Supplements and routines you record, your settings, and a cache of your synced data are kept in the app's private storage so it works offline. Access tokens on your phone are held in the iOS keychain.

Explanations written by AI

Some cards offer “Why is this?”. Before the first one, Strux asks your permission. If you agree, each time you tap it our server sends a short summary of your recent recovery, sleep and training figures to Anthropic, the company that makes the Claude AI model, which writes the explanation. Nothing else is sent: not your name, not your provider accounts, not your Apple Health data. Under Anthropic's commercial terms, this data is not used to train their models. If you never tap “Why is this?”, nothing is sent to Anthropic.

Crash reports

If Strux crashes, a report may be sent to Sentry, a crash-reporting service, so the fault can be fixed. A report contains the app version, your device model and iOS version, and technical details of the error. It does not contain your name, your health data or your IP address.

Who else receives your data

Only the companies above, and only to provide the features described: Cloudflare (hosting our server), Anthropic (explanations, only after you agree) and Sentry (crash reports). Strava, WHOOP and Oura receive requests from us for your data, under your authorisation. We do not sell or rent your data to anyone, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

Deleting your data

Disconnect a service in the Connect screen: its access tokens are removed from your phone and from our server, together with everything our server received from that service.

Delete your account in the Connect screen: our server deletes your account and everything stored with it — tokens, activities, recovery, sleep and usage counts. This cannot be undone.

Delete the app: removes everything stored on your phone. (Your anonymous account on our server stays until you delete it in the app, or ask us to.)

To revoke Strux's access at the source, independently of the app:

  • Strava — https://www.strava.com/settings/apps
  • OOP — the Connected Apps section of your WHOOP account
  • ra — the Connected apps section of your Oura account

How long we keep it

Until you disconnect the service it came from, or delete your account. Crash reports are kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.

Children

Strux is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly process their data.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, export or delete the data we hold about you. Deleting your account in the app does the last of these immediately. If you are in a jurisdiction granting rights over personal data — including the UK and EU under GDPR, and California under CCPA — those rights apply, and we will help you exercise them.

Changes

Material changes to this policy will be reflected here with an updated date, and made before the change in the app ships.
